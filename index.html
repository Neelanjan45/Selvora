About Selvora

Selvora is a fragrance brand that draws inspiration from nature and nostalgia. Our scents are crafted to evoke memories and emotions, transporting you to serene landscapes and cherished moments. Each fragrance is a blend of carefully selected ingredients, designed to create a unique olfactory experience that resonates with your soul.
